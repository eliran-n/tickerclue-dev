Application capabilities

Homepage search, sectors & favorites

  • Search a local catalog of 10,452 listed symbols by company name or ticker. The catalog includes stocks and other listed securities; data availability varies by symbol.
  • Browse all stocks, filter selected companies by sector or investment theme, or switch to Favorites to open a saved stock directly from the homepage.
  • Browser Back and Forward restore the selected stock, search filters, chart comparisons and view state using reusable loaded results.

Market data & trading sessions

  • Yahoo Finance quotes with daily price change, day low/high, 52-week range, market capitalization and volume, where available.
  • Price refresh available every five minutes, with update timestamps and a refresh countdown.
  • New York market-session indicators distinguish pre-market, regular trading, after-hours and closed sessions.

Company profile & business context

  • Company overview, sector and industry classification, main revenue sources and competitors.
  • Company context sits alongside the stock data, with separate views for the latest analysis and company profile.

Gemini-powered stock analysis

  • Google Gemini API integration generates concise explanations of the latest supplied trading session, with refresh available hourly after a successful analysis.
  • Up to five concise explanations of the stock’s latest movement distinguish market data, source-supported events and possible contributing factors.
  • Analysis retains its session date; closed-market notices prevent older price commentary from appearing to describe today's trading.

Source-linked news & article previews

  • Company-relevant articles from selected English and Hebrew financial publishers, filtered for source, relevance and duplicates.
  • Hover over a headline to view an available public article summary without opening the link; previews also support touch and keyboard interaction.
  • Hebrew news falls back to English when no valid Hebrew articles are found. Preview content depends on publicly available summaries.

Interactive price history

  • Explore one day, ten days, one month, year to date, one year and five years of available price history.
  • Switch between line, candlestick and bar views; inspect individual observations with pointer or touch interaction.
  • Optional S&P 500 comparison adds broader-market context.

Multi-stock return comparisons

  • Compare the selected company with up to two additional stocks, with an optional S&P 500 overlay.
  • Percentage returns start from a shared observed date or timestamp, rather than comparing unrelated price scales.
  • Choose comparison stocks through ticker search, favorites or similar-sector browsing; added stocks load price history independently of AI and news.

Analyst coverage & price targets

  • Recent investment-house recommendations include rating actions, publication dates and individual price targets where supplied.
  • Average analyst price target, coverage count and target upside/downside provide consensus context where available.
  • Duplicate investment houses are removed from recent recommendation cards; truncated rating actions remain accessible in full.

Valuation & financial health

  • Trailing and forward P/E, Price/Sales, Price/Book and EV/EBITDA, alongside year-over-year revenue and earnings growth.
  • Gross, operating and net margins, free cash flow, debt/equity and current ratio, where available.
  • Company-specific risk and opportunity indicators derive from available financial data, with missing fields explicitly identified.

Quarterly results & forward outlook

  • The last four reported quarters show revenue, net income or loss, and actual versus estimated EPS with earnings surprises where available.
  • Next-quarter revenue and EPS expectations appear separately from verified company guidance.
  • Unavailable guidance, missing metrics and analyst estimates are presented distinctly instead of filling gaps with assumed values.

Upcoming earnings & company events

  • Upcoming earnings dates and dated company events can include product launches, conferences and shareholder events.
  • Source links and verification labels distinguish supported events from estimated earnings dates.
  • Coverage depends on available sources; the interface handles sparse results and longer scrollable event lists.

Local favorites & device transfer

  • Add or remove favorites from search results or individual stock pages, with quick access from within the application.
  • Favorites stay in browser-local storage without a user account.
  • Explicit QR-code or link transfer moves a favorites list between devices, with confirmation before importing.

Implementation & design decisions

Responsive interface & localization

Desktop and mobile layouts, English LTR and Hebrew RTL support, light/dark themes, and language and appearance preferences saved across reloads. Keyboard navigation, accessible status messages and reduced-motion handling support everyday usability.

Independent data pipelines & caching

Price data, AI analysis, news, company guidance and events load independently. Browser-side reuse and Cloudflare Workers KV caching reduce repeat requests. If an AI refresh fails, the last successful analysis remains visible with its original timestamp; generation retries are bounded.

Architecture & development

A framework-free HTML, CSS and JavaScript frontend calls same-origin TypeScript Cloudflare Pages Functions. Provider API credentials remain on the server. The application is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with Gemini for AI analysis and Yahoo Finance for available market data.

Developed with OpenAI Codex and AI-assisted development, with personal ownership of the product requirements, interface design and feature refinement.
